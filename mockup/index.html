Case study

citizenM

Campaigns that generated €20M+ in revenue, the brand's best-performing to date, kept unmistakably citizenM on every surface.

Client
citizenM, global hotel brand
Role
Senior Digital Designer & Campaign Lead, Brand Studio
Deliverables
Campaigns, CRM, landing pages, paid, social, OOH, brand film
Impact
€20M+ campaign revenue
€50K+ saved a year on production

citizenM is a global hotel brand built on a strong visual identity and an irreverent tone of voice. As the in-house creative lead, the job was to carry that identity into every digital surface, from a CRM system reaching millions of subscribers to paid campaigns across Meta, Google and programmatic channels.

We built and maintained the brand's design system in Figma, set up templates the marketing team could scale, and directed a short brand film end to end. An in-house content team took over photography and short-form video from external agencies, saving more than €50K a year.

The approach

The work sat where brand, web and performance meet. Every landing page, CRM flow and campaign had to feel playful, bold and never self-serious, while also pulling its weight commercially. That meant a visual system that could flex across dozens of campaigns without losing coherence.

At its centre is a web design system in Figma: reusable components, templates, Auto Layout structures and design tokens. It gave the in-house team and external partners one foundation to work from, and let campaigns turn around fast without starting from scratch.

Testing as part of the creative loop

Campaign performance fed straight back into design. A/B tests, Hotjar heatmaps and Google Analytics showed which patterns actually converted, and the design system was sharpened on that evidence rather than on how a mockup looked.

Role

Leading the campaign team in citizenM's Brand Studio meant owning web design across the digital ecosystem: setting creative direction, defining quality benchmarks and keeping one visual language across every page and touchpoint, alongside running the A/B tests and iterating on conversion data.

Alongside the creative work: mentoring a junior designer, reviewing the team's output, and managing external agencies, photographers and production partners. Every launch was shaped with Marketing, Product and Content, with design decisions tied to business KPIs.
