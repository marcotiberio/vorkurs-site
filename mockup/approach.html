How we work. What we do, the ways you can bring us in, and the principles behind both.

Practice

Consulting & Strategy

We work out what needs to happen, and what doesn’t. Goals, users, platform and how your team actually works, so you invest in the right thing and everyone knows why.

Brand & Story Systems

Brand, content and digital systems that hold together in practice, not just on paper. Teams move faster, stay consistent, and change course without starting over.

Build & Deploy

We build fast, accessible, maintainable products and put them into production: websites, design systems, internal tools, AI-assisted workflows. No fragile setups, no handing off to someone else to finish.

Stewardship & Evolution

After launch we stay close. We watch how it’s used, improve what matters and keep the system from fragmenting, so your investment keeps paying off instead of needing a rebuild.

Engagements

Most long-term partnerships start embedded. If you're not sure which fits, start with an audit.

Audit Fixed scope

For when something feels off but you're not sure what.

We look at your current setup (platform, brand, workflows), map the gaps, and give you a clear plan. Fixed fee, no commitment beyond that.

Embedded Monthly retainer

For teams that need someone senior inside, not alongside.

We join your team 2–3 days a week, sit with the people who use what we build, and work directly in your stack: design, engineering, or both.

Project End to end

For when you need something designed, built and running.

From brief to launch and into production. Strategy, design, engineering, deployment: one team, no handoff gaps.

Advisory Fractional lead

For organisations that need senior direction without a full-time hire.

We come in as a fractional lead: set the direction, define the workflow, align the team, and step back when the system runs on its own.

Principles

The name comes from the Bauhaus Vorkurs, the preliminary course where every student started with materials, form and function. These four come from there.

Form follows function

Every decision is shaped by use, context, and accessibility. Structure comes first.

True materials

Strategy, technology, and content that reflect reality. Built for durability, not short-term appeal.

Systems over artifacts

We don't make one-off deliverables. We make structures that stay consistent as they grow.

Close to the problem

We work where the work happens: with your team, your users and your real data, not from a deck at a distance.
